# Storyboard

## Video direction

Realistic presenter-led civic film. The presenter is a fictional production role, not a named authority or endorsement. Use a licensed performer or clearly licensed synthetic presenter. Keep the Give A Bit family continuity in warmth, clarity, and proof-first honesty, but make SherpaCarta visually distinct with a constitutional forest/emerald/gold palette. Use clean cuts, gentle dissolves, one slow push-in at the opening, and an uplifting end-card lift. Captions are always safe on mobile.

## Audio direction

- Narration is primary and must remain intelligible on phone speakers.
- Use an upbeat, acoustic, shanty-inspired instrumental with no lyrics: hand percussion, light plucked strings, modest communal lift; avoid pirate parody, nationalism, or aggressive drops.
- Keep music low beneath speech, duck during every sentence, and fade gently under the final URL.
- No unlicensed music or generated voice/likeness may be published without documented rights.
