## Voiceover

Every age has to decide one thing: how do we keep power accountable?

### [0:07–0:17]
Eight hundred years ago, the Magna Carta helped establish a lasting principle: no ruler should stand above the law. Later, human-rights documents carried that idea across borders.

### [0:17–0:27]
Today, power also lives in platforms, databases, and algorithms—systems that can influence what we see, what we access, and how we are judged.

### [0:27–0:40]
SherpaCarta is a public, living charter for the digital age: a starting point for privacy, control of personal data, expression, access, explanation, appeal, and human dignity.

### [0:40–0:51]
It is secular, nonpartisan, and open to challenge. It is not current law. It is an invitation to read carefully, discuss peacefully, and help build better rules for what comes next.

### [0:51–1:00]
