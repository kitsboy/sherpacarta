# Independent legal review request

**Purpose:** request a written, scoped review of SherpaCarta materials. This is a template, not legal advice or evidence of review.

## Materials to provide

- Current charter version and complete SHA-256 hash
- `docs/FORMAL-DOCUMENT-PACKET.md`
- `docs/LEGAL-TRUTH-GUIDE.md`
- `docs/CANADA-JOURNEY.md`
- Public campaign configuration and privacy description
- Release manifest and approval record

## Questions for counsel

1. What legal status may the project accurately claim in the relevant jurisdiction?
2. Which words or calls to action create petition, solicitation, political advertising, charitable, privacy, or consumer-protection risk?
3. What disclosures are required for local signatures, optional remote receipt hashes, analytics, donations, and Nostr publication?
4. Is the Canada campaign clearly separated from an official House of Commons e-petition?
5. What process is required before using formal letterhead or submitting model language to a public authority?
6. What translation, accessibility, records, and consent standards apply?
7. What changes are required before public launch or institutional reliance?

## Required deliverable

A dated memo naming the lawyer/firm, jurisdictions, materials reviewed, assumptions, limitations, unanswered questions, and recommended changes. Store only an appropriately shareable version in the repository; keep privileged material private.
