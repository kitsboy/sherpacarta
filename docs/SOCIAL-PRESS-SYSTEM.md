# Social and press system

## Purpose

Every shared link should look deliberate and communicate the truth in one glance. Social previews are distribution surfaces, not evidence of legal status or endorsement.

## Required metadata

Each public landing page should have:

- canonical URL;
- Open Graph title, description, image, dimensions, and alt text;
- Twitter/X summary-large-image metadata;
- concise, platform-safe description;
- no private data in query strings;
- a visible route to source, proof, and legal/truth boundaries.

## Platforms

- **WhatsApp/Facebook:** use a 1200×630 JPEG/PNG with a short, human description and no unverified numbers.
- **X/Twitter:** keep title under practical display limits; lead with the document’s purpose, not legal claims.
- **Nostr:** use a concise note with canonical URL, hashtags, and explicit “not current law” language. Publishing must be user-authorized.
- **Press:** link to `/press-kit.html`, `/share.html`, the release manifest, and the current external-gates record.

## Demo-data rule

Sample people, quotes, endorsements, legal reviewers, MPs, approval records, metrics, or coalition maps must carry **DEMO DATA** and must never appear as real evidence in a final press release. “Steve Jobs” may be used only as a clearly labeled fictional example and must never imply a quote, endorsement, employment, approval, or relationship.

## Social workflow

1. Start from `/share.html` or `/press-kit.html`.
2. Copy the platform-specific text.
3. Confirm the linked release and status are current.
4. Remove any demo content before external publication.
5. Preserve the source and evidence links.
6. Correct publicly if a stale or misleading preview was shared.

## Review checklist

- Does the preview say what the document is?
- Does it avoid “official,” “law,” “approved,” “confirmed,” or “endorsed” without evidence?
- Does the image have strong contrast and readable type on mobile?
- Does the link resolve to the intended canonical route?
- Does the recipient have a path to verify the source and hash?
