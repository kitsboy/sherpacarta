# Operations evidence packet

## Government pathway

Keep the public campaign, paper petition, and official e-petition separate. Do not populate an e-### until Parliament publishes it. Evidence: official Parliament URL, identifier, sponsor, publication date, eligibility, and archived snapshot.

## Organizational endorsements

An endorsement is valid only with direct confirmation from an authorized representative. Record organization name, representative role, exact approved statement, scope, date, expiry/revocation contact, and public source. Do not infer endorsement from attendance, links, logos, or informal conversation.

## Treasury custody

Publish only public addresses and balances. Keep seeds, xprvs, invoices, admin tokens, and recovery material outside this repository. Before claiming multisig, record the chosen wallet design, quorum, signer separation, backup/recovery plan, test transaction, and responsible custodians in a protected operations system.

## Nostr operations

Keep nsecs and signing credentials in managed secret storage. Document key ownership, rotation, relay policy, moderation, incident response, backup, and shutdown. Public npubs and NIP-05 records are not proof of organizational authorization.

## Translation approval

For every language, record reviewer identity/qualification, source version/hash, coverage percentage, glossary, unresolved terms, review date, and approval scope. “UI available” or “community translation” is not professional or legal approval.

## Independent archive and recovery

Archive the source, generated exports, manifest, hash, release approvals, and build metadata at an independently controlled location. Test retrieval and restore from a clean environment; record checksum comparison, operator, date, failures, and corrective actions. An archive URL alone is not a recovery drill.
