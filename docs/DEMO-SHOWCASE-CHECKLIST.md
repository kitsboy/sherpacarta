# Demo showcase checklist

## Before showing anyone

- Open `/share.html` and confirm the preview/copy text.
- Open `/press-kit.html` and keep the **DEMO DATA** notice visible.
- Open `/data/external-gates.json` to show the uncompleted external items.
- Open `docs/DEEP-GAP-REGISTER.md` to explain repository versus external work.
- Do not replace demo names with real names until permission is obtained.

## What to show a lawyer

- `/verify.html`
- `docs/LEGAL-TRUTH-GUIDE.md`
- `docs/LEGAL-COUNSEL-REQUEST-TEMPLATE.md`
- Canada’s separate campaign, paper, and official routes
- The exact data fields and proof boundaries

## What to show a local leader

- `/start.html`
- `/amendments.html`
- `/canada/`
- `docs/GOVERNMENT-SPONSORSHIP-TEMPLATE.md`
- `docs/ENDORSEMENT-VERIFICATION-TEMPLATE.md`

## What to show a technical reviewer

- `SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md`
- `docs/IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md`
- `docs/THREAT-MODEL.md`
- `docs/BROWSER-ENDPOINT-TEST-PLAN.md`
- CI workflows and `npm run check:*` commands

## What to say plainly

“This package is designed to reveal the work still required. Demo records are labeled. No fictional person, lawyer, MP, organization, auditor, translator, custodian, or archive is being presented as real evidence.”
