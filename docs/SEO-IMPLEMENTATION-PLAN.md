# SEO, language, rights, and media implementation plan

**Updated:** 2026-08-26

## Implemented in this package

- Machine-readable multilingual SEO metadata for eight locales.
- Explicit secular/nonpartisan/non-current-law framing.
- Human-review flags for every non-source language.
- Rights taxonomy for discovery and education.
- Social sharing page and platform copy.
- Press-kit demo disclosures.
- Two enhanced one-minute video scripts and production handoff.
- Rights taxonomy and metadata contract checks.
- Service-worker coverage for new metadata.

## Still requires human review

- Native-language metadata review.
- Full charter translations.
- Legal review of sensitive rights terminology.
- RTL and long-text visual QA.
- Historical and legal source citation review.
- Final video editorial, legal, accessibility, and media-rights review.

## SEO principles

1. Discoverability must not outrun evidence.
2. “Magna Carta” is a secular historical analogy about limits on power.
3. Search copy must not imply the charter is enacted law.
4. No invented rankings, reach, countries, endorsements, or adoption.
5. Every page should offer source, release, hash, and truth-boundary links.
6. Translated pages must disclose coverage and review state.
