Governance

A living charter needs a visible process.

This page explains the intended amendment path. It is a governance design, not proof that a proposal has passed or that the charter has legal force.

01 · Propose

State the change

Describe the article, reason, affected people, evidence, and whether the change expands or narrows a protection.

02 · Discuss

Make disagreement visible

Publish accessible supporting and opposing reasoning, translation notes, and a clear review window.

03 · Record

Keep the history

Record the decision, outcome, release hash, and proof status. Do not erase prior versions or call an unconfirmed stamp confirmed.

Current status

No formal amendment approval authority is asserted by this website. Proposals, if published, require an identified process owner and a durable public record before they can be described as adopted.
