How Nostr works (30 seconds)

Keys, not accounts

You create a keypair. Public key = your address (npub…). Private key signs notes — never share it. Optional NIP-05 like sherpa@giveabit.io makes it human-readable.

Relays, not silos

Notes are JSON events pushed to relays (simple WebSocket servers). Clients fetch from many relays. The network is the product — not one database.

Clients compete

Damus, Primal, Amethyst, Snort… switch apps without losing followers. Competition improves UX; the protocol stays stable.
