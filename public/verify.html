Know what the proof actually says.

A SherpaCarta stamp links a specific document version to a cryptographic hash. Satohash.io can anchor that hash through Bitcoin. Anyone can inspect the proof; no account or trust in this page is required.

Stamp a document Open Satohash verify Security model
01 · CONTENT

Hash the version

A SHA-256 hash is a compact fingerprint of the exact text or file. Change the content and the fingerprint changes.

02 · ANCHOR

Stamp through Satohash

Satohash records the hash and submits timestamp evidence. The first result may be pending while Bitcoin confirmation is processed.

03 · CHECK

Verify independently

Open the proof, compare the hash, inspect its status, and use the downloadable evidence or external links where available.

Important distinction: a Bitcoin timestamp helps prove that a particular hash existed by a particular point in time. It does not prove that SherpaCarta is legislation, that every signer is verified, or that a pending stamp is confirmed. The site must always show those distinctions plainly.

A quick checklist

Before you rely on a proof

  • Confirm the document title and version match the material you received.
  • Compare the complete SHA-256 hash, not only a shortened display.
  • Check whether Satohash reports confirmed or pending.
  • Keep the proof URL and downloaded evidence with the document.
  • Remember: integrity and timestamp evidence are not the same as legal validity.

Recovery

If your proof is missing

If a proof link is expired, unavailable, or still pending, keep the original document, its complete hash, and any receipt ID. Recalculate the hash locally, check the live Satohash page, and contact support without sending private keys or identity documents.
