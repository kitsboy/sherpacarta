The path to e-###

1

Build mandate

Campaign signs + federal paper sheets. Public discussion on X (#Sherpacarta) and Nostr. This is where we are strongest today.

2

Create & certify the draft

Account on petitions.ourcommons.ca · paste federal prayer (≤250 words, no URLs) · 5 supporters confirm by email · Clerk review.

3

MP authorizes

A Member of Parliament must accept the invitation. That unlocks a public e-### on Parliament’s site.

4

500+ validated signatures

Email confirmation on ourcommons.ca. At threshold, petition can be presented; government responds within 45 days after presentation.

Rules that keep friction low

  • No minimum age to sign an e-petition
  • No government ID upload — email confirmation on Parliament’s site
  • Canadian citizen or resident (including citizens abroad)
  • 500 validated e-signatures to present; government responds within 45 days after presentation
  • Paper petitions: original ink, name + address, minimums for certification — print sheets

While we seek an MP

Do not wait. Campaign volume and paper are the argument. Public conversation keeps the issue warm.
