  • A Member of Parliament authorizes publication on petitions.ourcommons.ca

Rules that keep friction low

  • No minimum age to sign an e-petition
  • No government ID upload — email confirmation on Parliament’s site
  • Canadian citizen or resident (including citizens abroad)
  • 500 validated e-signatures to present; government responds within 45 days after presentation
  • Paper petitions: original ink, name + address, minimums for certification — print sheets
  • Prayer: ≤250 words, federal jurisdiction, no URLs, respectful language

Quick answers

Can SherpaCarta host the official signatures?

No. By design and by law of the process, official e-signatures only exist on Parliament’s website after e-### is published. We deep-link when live. Anything else would be dishonest.

Does asking my MP to authorize mean they support every article?

No. Authorization allows Canadians to sign on the official system. Our outreach draft says so. The charter is a reference framework, not enacted law.

What should I do if I’m not an “organizer”?

Sign the campaign, print or share a paper sheet, post the press line, and email your own MP. One polite constituent ask is a real contribution.

Where is the full legal model?
