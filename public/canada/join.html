Quick answers

Does scanning this QR count in Parliament?

No. Parliament counts original paper presented by an MP, or confirmed signatures on a live e-petition at petitions.ourcommons.ca. This QR is education + a separate public mandate.

I already signed online — do I still need ink?

Yes, if you want the sheet to work. Wet ink is what makes paper presentable. Online adds momentum and proof — it is not a substitute.

What am I actually supporting?

Stronger digital rights in Canada: privacy, data sovereignty, limits on mass and commercial surveillance, and fair algorithms — with SherpaCarta (114 articles, CC0) as a clear reference.

Who can join the digital mandate?

Canadian citizens and residents, including citizens abroad (self-attested). No government ID and no minimum age on the digital form. Official e-petition rules still apply on Parliament’s site when that path is live.

Will you sell or track my data?

No ad tech. The online form stores a receipt on your device and may sync only a hash, province, and method — not phone, street address, or ID documents.
