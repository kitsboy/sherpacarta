Quick questions

Does this QR sign the Parliamentary petition for me?

No. Parliament only counts original paper presented by an MP, or signatures on a live e-petition at petitions.ourcommons.ca after email confirmation. This QR opens education + a separate public mandate.

Should I still sign the paper if I sign online?

Yes. The wet signature is what makes the sheet useful for presentation. Online is extra momentum and proof — not a substitute.

What am I supporting?

Stronger digital rights in Canada: privacy, data sovereignty, limits on mass and commercial surveillance, and algorithmic fairness — using SherpaCarta (114 articles, CC0) as a reference framework.

Who can sign the digital mandate?

Canadian citizens and residents of Canada, including citizens abroad (self-attested). No government ID and no minimum age on the digital form. Official e-petition rules still apply on Parliament’s site when live.
