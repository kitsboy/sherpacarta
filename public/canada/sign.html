What it takes to get e-### live

  • Prayer text — short federal ask (≤250 words), already drafted for digital rights reform.
  • 5–10 supporters — emails of people who will back the petition on Parliament’s system (not just this page).
  • One MP authorizes — any Member of Parliament can open the door; they have limited time to respond, and organizers may try more than one.
  • Clerk certification — Parliament’s process, not ours. We deep-link when published.
  • 500+ validated e-signatures — each person confirms by email from Parliament. That number is separate from this page’s total.

Questions people actually ask

If I sign here, does Parliament count it?

No. This page is a public mandate. Parliament only counts signatures on a live e-petition at ourcommons.ca / petitions.parl.gc.ca, or original paper presented by an MP. We keep those numbers separate on purpose.

Who can sign the public mandate?

Canadian citizens and residents of Canada, including citizens living abroad (self-attested). No government ID and no minimum age for this page. Official e-petition rules on Parliament’s site still apply when that path is live.

What data do you store?

A receipt on your device (local storage). Optionally we sync a hash of your receipt, province/territory, method, and attestation flag — not phone, street address, or ID documents. Optional wall display only if you check that box.

What is the “e-petition upgrade”?

It means moving from public support numbers to a formal House of Commons e-petition (e-###): MP authorization, Clerk process, then 500 confirmed signatures on Parliament’s website. This site cannot host official e-signatures for you.

Can I start the e-petition myself?

Yes — any Canadian who meets Parliament’s rules can create a petition on the official portal, gather supporters, and invite an MP. Use our drafted federal prayer and briefing so the ask is ready. Start at the portal link above, then use /canada/official for tools and checklist.

What does “Stamp hash” mean?

Optional proof: open Satohash with a SHA-256 of the petition text or your device’s merkle root. “Pending” is not the same as Bitcoin-confirmed. It is cryptographic integrity — not a Parliamentary signature.
