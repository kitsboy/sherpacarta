Quick answers

Is signing here the same as a House of Commons petition?

No — and we say so clearly. Campaign signs build mandate. Official e-signatures only count on petitions.ourcommons.ca after an MP publishes an e-###. Paper sheets are a separate legal path an MP can present.

Why participate before e-### is live?

MPs authorize petitions when they see demand. Campaign volume, paper ink, and public conversation are the argument. Waiting for Parliament first is how issues stay invisible.

Do you need my government ID or phone?

Not for the campaign. Campaign form: name or pseudonym, province, citizen/resident attestation. Official e-petitions (on Parliament’s site) use email confirmation — still no ID upload.

What happens if we get an official e-petition?

At 500+ validated signatures it can be presented in the House; the government must respond within 45 days after presentation. That is formal leverage — which is why we treat the path carefully and honestly.

Where can I read the full legal model?

See CANADA-PETITION-LEGAL.md and the journey notes in CANADA-JOURNEY.md. Not legal advice.
