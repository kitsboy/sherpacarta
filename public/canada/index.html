Your data is not a product

Stand up for digital rights in Canada

Mass surveillance, opaque algorithms, and surveillance advertising are rewriting power over ordinary life. SherpaCarta is a living Digital Magna Carta — 114 articles Canadians can organize around. This hub is how we turn that charter into public mandate and, with an MP, an official House of Commons e-petition.

The promise

Every campaign sign, paper sheet, and public post builds the case that Canadians want privacy by design, data sovereignty, and algorithmic accountability. We do not fake Parliamentary numbers. We build real pressure — then convert it into official process when a Member of Parliament sponsors us.

Privacy is a birthright

PIPEDA modernization, limits on mass surveillance, and a practical right to erasure are not niche tech issues — they are civil liberties.

Algorithms decide lives

Jobs, credit, content, and policing increasingly run on automated scores. You deserve plain-language explanation and non-discrimination.

Mandate moves MPs

Paper sheets and campaign commitments show demand. Official e-petitions need that signal — and an MP who will authorize e-###.
